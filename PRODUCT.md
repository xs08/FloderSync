# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who want local Git repositories to stay synchronized with minimal interruption. They use FloderSync as a background menu bar utility and open its settings window to configure repositories, automation, diagnostics, language, and appearance.

## Product Purpose

FloderSync makes conservative Git synchronization dependable and understandable. Success means repositories stay current automatically, important states remain visible, and failures provide clear recovery paths without hiding or damaging local work.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, native, professional. The interface should feel calm and precise, with enough visual depth to remain comfortable during focused use.

## Anti-references

- Flat near-black interfaces whose sidebar, content area, and controls collapse into one surface.
- Decorative glass effects, excessive translucency, or saturated color used without semantic purpose.
- Custom interactions that fight familiar macOS controls or reduce keyboard and assistive-technology support.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve native macOS behavior and familiar control vocabulary.
2. Use hierarchy to make repository state and next actions immediately understandable.
3. Keep automation dependable and quiet until attention is required.
4. Let visual refinement support the workflow instead of becoming decoration.
5. Keep appearance, language, and interaction states consistent across every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast targets for text and essential controls. Preserve clear keyboard focus, VoiceOver labels, non-color state cues, readable Simplified Chinese and English layouts, and reduced-motion-friendly behavior.
